## 3. EXISTING BRAND IDENTITY

The existing identity must be preserved, not redesigned.

- Charcoal / near-black background
- Gold lettering (#C69A45) for the SIWI wordmark
- Ivory (#F5F2EB) or metallic-white for supporting text
- Serif SIWI wordmark
- Globe enclosed within a directional compass motif
- Tagline: "Serving with trust, delivering with quality."

Do not change the company name. Do not introduce a new visual identity.

---

## 4. CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY — NON-NEGOTIABLE

The website must immediately communicate five things:

1. **Trust** — a credible company international buyers can contact with confidence
2. **Clarity** — visitors understand what SIWI exports and how to enquire
3. **Quality** — products and communication are presented professionally
4. **Global reach** — connects Indian products with international markets
5. **Reliability** — the website feels organised, precise, and easy to navigate

**Primary principle:** Design with fewer elements, but give every element a clear reason to exist.

Premium design comes from proportion, typography, contrast, image selection, alignment, whitespace, and the confidence to remove unnecessary elements — not from adding more visual effects.

---

## 5. NON-NEGOTIABLE DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

These apply to every page, every component, and every design decision:

1. Every page must have its own visual composition while remaining part of one coherent design system.
2. Do not fill empty spaces merely because they appear empty.
3. Do not make every section visually identical.
4. Do not force every page into the same hero-heading-image-card layout.
5. Do not use purple-to-blue gradients, random green accents, blurry gradient blobs, or glowing orbs.
6. Do not use generic corporate imagery (handshakes, office meetings, cargo ships used decoratively).
7. Do not fabricate product specifications, certifications, testimonials, export figures, or statistics.
8. Do not use shopping-cart or consumer e-commerce patterns. This is a B2B enquiry website.
9. Do not create a dark mode that simply inverts the light mode.
10. All navigation links, CTAs, and forms must be functional — no # placeholder links.

## 8. HOW TO USE THIS REFERENCE SYSTEM WITH STITCH

Google Stitch AI works best with focused, relevant reference material per task.

**Do not paste all 7 levels into a single prompt.**

Instead, follow the staged workflow in LEVEL_07_STITCH_EXECUTION_PLAYBOOK.md:

1. Upload or paste relevant level documents as context for each design stage.
2. Always include this master brief (LEVEL 0) as the first reference.
3. Add LEVEL 1 and LEVEL 2 to every prompt.
4. Add the task-specific levels (LEVEL 3, 4, 5, 6) only when relevant.
5. Use the pre-written prompts in the PROMPTS/ directory as your starting point.
6. Review and approve output before proceeding to the next stage.

Stitch may not have automatic access to local files. When it does not, paste the relevant level content directly into the prompt context.

## 10. CONTENT ACCURACY RULES

These rules are absolute and override all design preferences:

- Do not invent certifications (ISO, FSSAI, HACCP, or any other).
- Do not invent export destinations or client names.
- Do not invent product specifications (moisture %, shelf life, MOQ, packaging weight).
- Do not invent company milestones, years of experience, or turnover figures.
- Do not invent manufacturing facilities or warehouse locations.
- Do not fabricate customer testimonials or ratings.
- Do not invent a founder biography beyond what has been confirmed.
- Do not display a social media link unless the company has confirmed the actual URL.
- If information is missing, design the interface to accommodate verified information later.
