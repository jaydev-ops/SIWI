# 27. COPYWRITING — HUMAN, PRECISE, AND CREDIBLE

All website copy must sound like it was written by someone who understands international B2B trade.

Avoid empty corporate language.

## Do not use phrases such as

- "Revolutionising the global export ecosystem."
- "Empowering businesses through cutting-edge solutions."
- "Your one-stop destination for excellence."
- "Unlocking limitless possibilities."
- "Delivering innovation at scale."
- "Where quality meets innovation."
- "Your trusted partner in a rapidly evolving world."
- "Redefining the future of global commerce."

These phrases are generic and do not communicate meaningful information about SIWI Global Ventures.

## Prefer direct language

Instead of:
> "We leverage a comprehensive ecosystem to facilitate seamless global business transformation."

Write:
> "We connect Indian products with international buyers through dependable trade partnerships."

Instead of:
> "Experience unparalleled quality and excellence."

Write:
> "We focus on product quality, competitive pricing, and dependable service."

Instead of:
> "Unlock your business's global potential."

Write:
> "Tell us what products you need and what your business requires."

## Copywriting principles

- Be specific.
- Be concise.
- Prefer active voice.
- Use natural sentence structures.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Avoid repetitive keywords.
- Avoid overly formal corporate language.
- Avoid unnecessary exclamation marks.
- Never invent proof of quality.
- Never claim the company is a market leader without evidence.
- Never fabricate customer testimonials.
- Never invent export destinations.
- Never promise delivery times that have not been confirmed.

The website should feel confident because it communicates clearly, not because it makes exaggerated promises.
