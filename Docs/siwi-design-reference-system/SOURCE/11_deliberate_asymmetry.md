# 11. DELIBERATE ASYMMETRY

Use asymmetry to make the website feel art-directed.

Avoid mechanically repeating identical sections from top to bottom.

## Examples

**Homepage**
A large headline aligned to the left, with a vertically offset product image to the right.

**Products**
A wide image occupying roughly 60% of the layout, with product information in a narrower column.

**About**
A long-form company introduction paired with a small, carefully positioned brand illustration.

**Trade capabilities**
A numbered vertical list on one side and supporting explanatory content on the other.

**Contact**
A spacious contact-information column beside a focused enquiry form.

## Rules

- Maintain an underlying grid.
- Make asymmetry intentional.
- Preserve clear reading order.
- Avoid arbitrary floating elements.
- Do not compromise accessibility.
- Ensure mobile layouts remain straightforward.

Asymmetry must improve the composition, not make the website difficult to understand.
