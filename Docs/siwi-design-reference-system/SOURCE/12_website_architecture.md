# 12. COMPLETE WEBSITE ARCHITECTURE

Design the entire website as a connected multi-page experience.

Do not create a single long landing page with every page represented by a section.

Each primary navigation item must lead to its own dedicated page with a clear purpose, suitable content, and appropriate layout.

## Page routes

| Page | Route | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Home | / | Introduce SIWI and guide visitors towards products and enquiries |
| About Us | /about | Explain the company, its approach, and its founder |
| Products | /products | Present the product catalogue |
| Dehydrated Vegetables | /products/dehydrated-vegetables | Present the dehydrated vegetable category |
| Onion Powder | /products/onion-powder | Provide product-specific information |
| Garlic Powder | /products/garlic-powder | Provide product-specific information |
| Garlic Granules | /products/garlic-granules | Provide product-specific information |
| Dehydrated Fried Onions | /products/dehydrated-fried-onions | Provide product-specific information |
| Birista | /products/birista | Provide product-specific information |
| Onion Kibbles | /products/onion-kibbles | Provide product-specific information |
| Tiles | /products/tiles | Present the secondary trading category |
| Other Products | /products/other-products | Accommodate verified additional offerings |
| Trade Enquiries | /trade-enquiries | Explain the enquiry process and capture buyer requirements |
| Contact | /contact | Provide direct contact information and an enquiry form |
| Privacy Policy | /privacy-policy | Explain relevant personal-data practices |
| Terms | /terms | Present applicable website terms |

Product detail pages should share reusable templates, but their imagery, descriptions, and content must reflect the individual product.

If insufficient information exists to justify a complete product detail page, create a well-designed provisional page that clearly invites enquiries. Do not invent specifications to fill space.

The Tiles page must be visually and structurally connected to the rest of the website while remaining clearly distinct from the dehydrated vegetable category.
