# Premium International Trade & Export Website — Complete Design System and Multi-Page Experience

## 1. YOUR ROLE

Act as a senior digital art director, brand identity designer, UX architect, typography specialist, and frontend design engineer.

Design a complete, production-quality, multi-page corporate website for SIWI Global Ventures, an India-based international trading and export company specialising in dehydrated vegetable products and other traded goods.

The website must establish credibility with international buyers, importers, distributors, food manufacturers, wholesalers, and business partners.

This is not a generic corporate website.

It must feel like a carefully considered international trading company with a strong identity, professional standards, clear communication, and an understanding of global business.

The visual quality should combine:
- Apple's restrained typography and precise spatial composition.
- The editorial sophistication of premium international brands.
- The credibility and clarity of established B2B companies.
- The precision of modern industrial and logistics design.
- The warmth and material character of the company's existing black, gold, and ivory branding.

The final result should look designed by a human art director, not assembled by an AI website generator.

Avoid predictable templates, excessive cards, repetitive rounded containers, generic gradients, decorative blobs, and the usual AI-generated SaaS aesthetic.

Every page must have its own visual composition while remaining part of one coherent design system.
