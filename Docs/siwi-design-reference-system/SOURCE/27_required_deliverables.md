# 36. REQUIRED DELIVERABLES

Generate the following as part of the project.

1. **Complete multi-page website** — All defined pages must exist as separate routes, with functional navigation between them.
2. **Light and dark themes** — Both themes must be fully designed and usable.
3. **Responsive layouts** — Desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts must be considered explicitly.
4. **Reusable design system** — Typography, colours, spacing, buttons, navigation, product templates, and form elements must follow consistent rules.
5. **Product catalogue** — Create a structured catalogue covering the confirmed products and categories.
6. **Individual product pages** — Provide appropriate product detail templates and enquiry functionality.
7. **Functional enquiry experience** — Build the enquiry and contact forms, including validation, feedback, and a real submission mechanism where supported.
8. **Brand consistency** — Use the supplied SIWI Global Ventures logo and retain the existing visual identity.
9. **Production-conscious implementation** — Ensure the website is maintainable, accessible, responsive, and optimised for performance.
10. **Content integrity** — Do not invent company facts or product specifications.
11. **SEO foundations** — Include page metadata, appropriate internal linking, and other relevant SEO fundamentals.
12. **Final quality review** — Check the website for broken routes, non-functional buttons, responsive issues, inconsistent spacing, unreadable text, and visual repetition.

Do not stop after generating the homepage.

Do not present a design system without implementing the pages.

Do not replace individual pages with anchors that scroll to sections on the homepage.

Build the complete website as one coherent, multi-page brand experience.

## Recommended visual direction

For SIWI, prioritise these three design decisions above everything else.

### 1. Keep the gold-and-charcoal branding, but use ivory as the main reading surface

The business card already establishes a distinctive visual identity. The website should retain that identity without making every page dark.

Use:
- **Light mode:** warm ivory backgrounds, near-black typography, fine gold accents.
- **Dark mode:** deep charcoal backgrounds, ivory typography, restrained gold highlights.

This gives the brand flexibility while retaining recognition.

### 2. Make product photography the main visual asset

SIWI's business is about products and international trade. The website should show visitors what the company actually deals in.

A carefully photographed collection of dehydrated onion products, garlic powder, garlic granules, and other confirmed products will contribute more to credibility than dozens of abstract illustrations.

The existing compass logo already gives a distinctive brand element — an elaborate animated globe on every page is not needed.

### 3. Build the website around enquiries, not shopping

The primary conversion should be a qualified business enquiry.

A visitor should be able to find a product, understand the available information, and contact SIWI with minimal friction.

The most important interactions are:
- Explore Products.
- View Product Details.
- Enquire About This Product.
- Discuss Your Requirements.
- Call SIWI.
- Email SIWI.

Everything else should support those actions.

The brand name, logo, and tagline already establish a useful starting point. The biggest opportunity is to combine that existing identity with excellent product photography, Apple-inspired typography, editorial layouts, and genuinely useful product enquiry pages. That combination will feel far more professional than adding more animations or decorative effects.
