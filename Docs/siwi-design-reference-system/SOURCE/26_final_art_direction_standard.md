# 35. FINAL ART-DIRECTION STANDARD

Before finalising the design, evaluate every page against the following criteria.

## Visual identity

- Does the website clearly belong to SIWI Global Ventures?
- Is the black, gold, and ivory identity used consistently?
- Does the compass-and-globe motif feel distinctive rather than decorative?
- Does the website avoid generic green corporate branding?

## Typography

- Does the typography feel restrained and carefully selected?
- Are the headings proportioned correctly?
- Is the body text readable?
- Does the layout use whitespace instead of excessive boldness?

## Layout

- Is there enough whitespace?
- Are the margins and alignments consistent?
- Does each page have its own composition?
- Is asymmetry intentional?
- Are cards used only when necessary?

## Content

- Is the company's primary business immediately clear?
- Are the products presented accurately?
- Does the copy sound natural?
- Are all claims supported by the supplied information?
- Are missing specifications handled honestly?

## Functionality

- Does every page have a clear purpose?
- Do all links and buttons work?
- Can buyers enquire about individual products?
- Do the forms validate correctly?
- Do dark mode and light mode both work?
- Is the mobile navigation accessible?

## Professional quality

The result should look like a custom-designed website for an established B2B trading company.

It should not look like a generic Shopify storefront, a SaaS dashboard, an AI startup landing page, or a prebuilt export-company template.

The defining qualities must be clarity, restraint, precise typography, confident whitespace, product relevance, and credible communication.
