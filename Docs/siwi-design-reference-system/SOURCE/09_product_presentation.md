# 9. PRODUCT PRESENTATION — NO GENERIC E-COMMERCE GRID

SIWI Global Ventures is a B2B export business.

Its product catalogue should not resemble an online shopping marketplace.

Visitors need to understand the range, assess relevance, and enquire about supply requirements.

They do not need shopping carts, consumer ratings, prices beside every product, or unnecessary purchase buttons.

## Preferred presentation

Use editorial product listings, not repetitive cards.

**Example:**

**Dehydrated Onion Products**

*Onion Powder* — A versatile dehydrated ingredient for suitable food-processing and culinary applications.

*Onion Kibbles* — Dehydrated onion pieces for relevant commercial food applications.

*Dehydrated Fried Onions* — Fried onion products for suitable food-service and food-manufacturing requirements.

**Dehydrated Garlic Products**

*Garlic Powder* — A convenient dehydrated garlic format for appropriate food applications.

*Garlic Granules* — A granular format for buyers seeking a different texture and handling profile.

**Other Products**

*Birista* — Dehydrated and fried onion products for relevant commercial requirements.

*Other Value-Added Products* — Additional products subject to availability and confirmation.

These descriptions are illustrative starting points. Verify product formats and intended applications before publishing final copy.

## Layout options

Use one of the following patterns where appropriate:
- Large product image on one side, product information on the other.
- Editorial list with product names and short descriptions.
- Asymmetric two-column composition.
- A large category introduction followed by a compact product list.
- Alternating image-and-text layouts for important product families.

Do not force all product categories into identical cards.

## Product detail pages

Each confirmed product should have its own detail page when enough information exists to justify it.

A product detail page may include:
1. Product name.
2. Product photograph.
3. Short description.
4. Available product formats.
5. Available packaging options, once confirmed.
6. Relevant product specifications, once confirmed.
7. Storage or handling information, where verified.
8. A clear enquiry CTA.
9. Related products.
10. A direct contact option.

**Do not fabricate:**
- Moisture percentages.
- Shelf life.
- Microbiological specifications.
- Packaging weights.
- Minimum order quantities.
- Certifications.
- Food safety compliance claims.
- Manufacturing processes.
- Origin certifications.

If a specification has not been supplied, display an appropriate enquiry option rather than an invented value.

**CTA:** Enquire About This Product

This is more suitable than "Buy Now."
