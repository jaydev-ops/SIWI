# 26. IMAGE ART DIRECTION

Images should reinforce the company's products and commercial identity.

Use an editorial approach rather than filling the website with unrelated stock photographs.

## Preferred subjects

- Close-up onion powder textures.
- Garlic powder and garlic granules.
- Dehydrated onion pieces.
- Fried onion products.
- Verified product packaging.
- Ingredients arranged on neutral surfaces.
- Subtle geographical or trade-related visuals where appropriate.

## Lighting

Use soft directional lighting, realistic shadows, and natural material detail.

## Composition

Prefer:
- Strong focal points.
- Cropped editorial compositions.
- Asymmetric framing.
- Natural texture.
- Neutral backgrounds.
- Negative space for typography.

## Avoid

- Generic business handshakes.
- Random cargo ships used as decoration.
- Stock photos of office meetings.
- AI-generated businesspeople.
- Unrealistic product textures.
- Fake certificates.
- Fabricated packaging.
- Images that do not correspond to the product being described.

## Image consistency

Maintain a consistent photographic language across the website.

Do not mix glossy consumer-advertising images, cartoon illustrations, generic corporate stock photography, and unrelated 3D renders.

If actual product photography is unavailable, use a small number of carefully selected, accurate ingredient images.
