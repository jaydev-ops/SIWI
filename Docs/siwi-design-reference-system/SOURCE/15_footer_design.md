# 24. FOOTER DESIGN

The footer should feel like a natural conclusion to the website.

Use a dark charcoal background in light mode and a slightly differentiated dark surface in dark mode, maintaining sufficient contrast.

## Footer composition

Create an asymmetric layout.

**Left column**
- SIWI Global Ventures logo.
- Tagline: Serving with trust, delivering with quality.
- A short description of the company's international trade focus.

**Middle column**

Explore:
- About Us.
- Products.
- Trade Enquiries.
- Contact.

**Right column**

Contact:
- +91 83696 35619.
- siwiglobalventure@gmail.com.
- Mumbai, India.

**Bottom row**

Include:
- Copyright notice.
- Privacy Policy.
- Terms.

Use fine separators and generous spacing.

Avoid a large collection of social icons or decorative widgets.

Do not invent social accounts.
