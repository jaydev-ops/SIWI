# 8. HERO SECTION — ART-DIRECTED, NOT TEMPLATE-DRIVEN

The homepage hero is the most important visual decision.

It must not resemble a standard AI-generated landing page with a centred heading, two buttons, a gradient background, and a floating dashboard mockup.

## Composition

Create an asymmetric split composition.

**On desktop:**
- Position the headline towards the left.
- Allow the text to occupy approximately 45–55% of the content width.
- Use the remaining space for a carefully art-directed visual.
- Align the image and text according to a considered grid.
- Use negative space intentionally.
- Avoid perfectly centred, overly symmetrical layouts.

The hero visual may combine:
- A close-up photograph of real dehydrated onion or garlic products.
- A refined editorial photograph of dehydrated vegetable ingredients.
- A subtle directional graphic derived from the compass logo.
- A restrained charcoal or ivory background.

The image should feel photographic and material, with natural texture, realistic lighting, and deliberate composition.

Avoid generic images of office buildings, handshakes, business meetings, random container ships, or smiling corporate teams.

## Product photography direction

Prioritise genuine, relevant images of:
- Onion powder.
- Garlic powder.
- Garlic granules.
- Dehydrated fried onions.
- Onion kibbles.
- Other verified products from the actual range.

Use macro photography, natural ingredient textures, restrained lighting, and neutral surfaces.

Do not represent one product as another.

Do not use AI-generated packaging bearing fabricated product labels, certifications, or company claims.

If genuine product images are unavailable, use restrained ingredient photography with accurate descriptions.

Do not make the absence of product photography look like a design failure.

## Hero content

- **Eyebrow:** INDIA · INTERNATIONAL TRADE
- **Headline:** Indian products. Global possibilities.
- **Body:** "Connecting quality Indian products with international markets through dependable trade partnerships."
- **Primary CTA:** Explore Our Products
- **Secondary CTA:** Discuss Your Requirements
- **Optional microcopy:** "Based in Mumbai, India."

Do not add fabricated client logos, customer ratings, export figures, or statistics.

## Mobile hero

On mobile:
- Stack content vertically.
- Keep the headline prominent without consuming the entire initial viewport.
- Place the primary CTA close to the supporting copy.
- Use a cropped image with a clear focal point.
- Preserve the logo and navigation.
- Prevent text from overlapping the hero image.
- Do not shrink the desktop layout into an unreadable miniature.
