# 34. WHAT MUST NOT APPEAR IN THE FINAL DESIGN

This section is mandatory.

Do not generate a website that looks like a typical AI-made corporate template.

## Strictly avoid

### Generic AI aesthetics
- Purple-to-blue gradients.
- Random green accents.
- Blurry gradient blobs.
- Glowing orbs.
- Glassmorphism everywhere.
- Unnecessary 3D objects.
- Floating dashboard mockups.
- Repetitive three-card sections.
- Excessive rounded rectangles.
- Random decorative icons.
- Identical layouts across every page.

### Generic corporate imagery
- Unrelated handshakes.
- Stock photographs of business meetings.
- Smiling people in suits.
- Random skyscrapers.
- Generic cargo ships used without purpose.
- Decorative shipping containers with no relationship to the content.

### Overdesigned interfaces
- Excessive pill buttons.
- Too many borders.
- Oversized shadows.
- Multiple competing CTAs.
- Excessive uppercase text.
- Too many font families.
- Decorative numbers with no informational value.
- Artificial statistics.
- Fake testimonials.
- Fake customer logos.
- Fake certifications.

### Poor typography
- Oversized bold text everywhere.
- Extremely wide letter spacing in body copy.
- Tiny body text.
- Unnecessary italics.
- Multiple competing display fonts.
- Generic font combinations chosen without a clear hierarchy.

### Unnecessary interactions
- Custom cursors.
- Scroll hijacking.
- Endless parallax.
- Autoplay video backgrounds.
- Constantly rotating globe animations.
- Unnecessary loading screens.
- Animations that delay access to content.

### Misleading business content
- Fabricated product specifications.
- Invented export destinations.
- Fake company milestones.
- Unverified certifications.
- Unverified manufacturing capabilities.
- Unsupported quality guarantees.
- Invented customer testimonials.

The website should be restrained enough to feel believable and distinctive enough to be memorable.
