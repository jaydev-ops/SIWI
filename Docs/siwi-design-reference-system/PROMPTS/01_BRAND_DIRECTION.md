# STITCH TASK PROMPT 01 — BRAND DIRECTION & MOODBOARD
## SIWI Global Ventures Design Execution

---

## 1. TASK ASSIGNMENT
Establish the primary visual direction and art-directed moodboard for SIWI Global Ventures, an India-based international trading company exporting dehydrated vegetable products (onion & garlic) and other traded goods (tiles).

The goal is to establish a high-end visual tone combining Apple-inspired typographic restraint, editorial sophistication, and B2B trade authority.

## 3. SELF-CONTAINED TASK BRIEF (FOR DIRECT PASTE)
If Stitch cannot access local files, copy and paste this context directly:

> **Project Identity:** SIWI Global Ventures  
> **Business:** Export of dehydrated vegetable products (Onion powder, Garlic powder, Garlic granules, Dehydrated fried onions, Birista, Onion kibbles) and secondary trading in Tiles.  
> **Brand Palette:** Dark charcoal/ink (`#111318`, `#191C21`), metallic gold (`#C69A45`), warm ivory (`#F5F2EB`), neutral greys. (Green is strictly prohibited as a brand color).  
> **Typography:** Clean Apple-inspired system sans-serif (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro"`) paired with a restrained editorial serif (`Cormorant Garamond` or `Libre Baskerville`) used sparingly for the SIWI wordmark and select titles.  
> **Signature Motif:** Vector hairline compass-and-globe emblem representing global trade routes and precision.  
> **Aesthetic Standard:** Spacious, deliberate whitespace, 8px grid, minimal cards, macro ingredient photography, zero generic SaaS clichés.

---

## 4. CONSTRAINTS & PROHIBITIONS
- Strictly NO generic corporate green accents.
- Strictly NO purple-to-blue gradients, glowing orbs, or floating cartoon 3D objects.
- Strictly NO stock photos of handshakes, smiling people in suits, or random container ships.
- Strictly NO consumer e-commerce shopping carts or "Add to Cart" buttons.

---

## 5. EXPECTED DELIVERABLES
Generate a high-fidelity visual moodboard presenting:
1. **Color Harmony Board:** Proportional application of charcoal, ivory, and gold (65–75% neutral, 15–25% secondary, 5–10% gold).
2. **Typographic Specimens:** Display headline scale, body copy pairing, and letter-spaced metadata labels.
3. **Hero Art-Direction Concept:** Asymmetric composition featuring macro dehydrated onion/garlic ingredient texture and fine compass geometry.
4. **Interactive Component Primitives:** Button variants, subtle border dividers, and tag badges.

---

## 6. REVIEW & APPROVAL CHECKLIST
- [ ] Does the mood feel like an established, premium international trading firm?
- [ ] Is green completely absent from the branding?
- [ ] Is the gold applied with subtlety (fine rules, accents) rather than gaudy solid blocks?
- [ ] Does the typography exhibit restraint and generous negative space?
