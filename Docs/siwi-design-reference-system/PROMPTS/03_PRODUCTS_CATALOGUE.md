# STITCH TASK PROMPT 03 — PRODUCTS CATALOGUE
## SIWI Global Ventures Design Execution

---

## 1. TASK ASSIGNMENT
Design the master Products Catalogue page (`/products`) for SIWI Global Ventures.

The catalogue must be engineered for international B2B buyers evaluating commercial commodity lines, providing clear category navigation and frictionless paths to detailed specifications and inquiries.

---

---

## 3. SELF-CONTAINED TASK BRIEF (FOR DIRECT PASTE)
> **Page Route:** `/products`  
> **Headline:** `A considered range of products for global trade.`  
> **Supporting Copy:** *"Explore our dehydrated vegetable products and other traded categories. For product specifications, availability, and commercial requirements, contact our team."*  
> **Category Structure:**  
> - **Primary Category (Dehydrated Vegetables):** Onion Powder, Garlic Powder, Garlic Granules, Dehydrated Fried Onions, Birista, Onion Kibbles, Other Value-Added Products. Each item must feature authentic macro photography, commercial description, format summary, and a `View Product Details` link.  
> - **Secondary Category (Other Traded Categories):** Tiles (ceramic/porcelain) and Other Products.  
> **Procurement CTA:** `Enquire About This Category` or `Request Commercial Quotation`.

---

## 4. CONSTRAINTS & NEGATIVE RULES
- Do NOT design this as a consumer e-commerce store (NO prices, NO shopping carts, NO "Add to Cart").
- Do NOT fabricate unconfirmed specifications like moisture %, mesh sizes, or minimum order quantities.
- Avoid repetitive card grids; use alternating visual rows, editorial lists, and generous whitespace.

---

## 5. EXPECTED DELIVERABLES
1. Full desktop layout of `/products` with category filtering tabs.
2. Category showcase presentation for the 7 dehydrated vegetable products.
3. Secondary category presentation for Tiles and Other Products.
4. Hover states and link pathways to individual product detail pages.

---
