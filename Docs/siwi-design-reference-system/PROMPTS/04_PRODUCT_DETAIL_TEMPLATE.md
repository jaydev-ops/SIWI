## 1. TASK ASSIGNMENT
Design the master reusable layout template for SIWI Global Ventures' individual product detail pages (e.g. `/products/onion-powder`).

The template must balance macro product presentation, industrial application data, packaging standards, and direct commercial enquiry initiation.

## 3. SELF-CONTAINED TASK BRIEF (FOR DIRECT PASTE)
> **Target Route:** Master framework for `/products/[slug]` (demonstrated with Onion Powder)  
> **Key Sections:**  
> - **Breadcrumbs:** `Home → Products → Dehydrated Vegetables → Onion Powder`  
> - **Hero Split:** Left: high-res macro ingredient photography (fine golden-white onion powder). Right: Product title, subtitle, origin tag (`Origin: India`), overview text, and primary CTA: `Discuss This Product` linking to `/trade-enquiries?product=onion-powder`.  
> - **Product Formats & Processing:** Clean editorial description of dehydration process and particle consistency.  
> - **Industrial Applications:** Seasonings, spice blends, dry soup formulations, meat processing, culinary services.  
> - **Technical Specifications Table:** Verified parameters or mandatory fallback: *"Contact our technical sourcing team for comprehensive specification sheets, microbiological parameters, and certificates of analysis."*  
> - **Export Packaging:** Bulk multi-wall paper bags, corrugated export cartons, customized labeling upon agreement.  
> - **Related Products Strip:** Cross-links to Garlic Powder and Onion Kibbles.  
> - **Bottom Conversion Bar:** Direct CTA to trade enquiries with pre-filled parameter.

---

## 4. CONSTRAINTS & NEGATIVE RULES
- Never fabricate lab data (microbial limits, moisture %, ash content) without verification.
- Never include consumer checkout or payment options.
- The enquiry button MUST link to `/trade-enquiries?product=[slug]` to enable pre-filling.
